The ritual

The Shark Service Standard

Every visit, every company, in this order. The CRM sends steps 1, 6 and 7 for you, so techs don't have to remember them.

Step 1 · 30 min out

"Fin's up" text

Photo of the tech, first name, rank and van. "Easton is on the way. Look for the orange stripe."

Step 2 · Arrival

Knock, intro, shoe covers

Uniform tucked, cap on. Covers go on before anyone steps inside. Hand over the Fin Check card.

Step 3 · Before

Walkaround

Walk the job together for two minutes. Point out problem spots, set expectations, take before photos.

Step 4 · Work

Do the job, leave it cleaner

Drop cloths down, tools on a mat, nothing on the lawn. Clean up beyond the job area.

Step 5 · After

Final Fin Check

Walk it again with the customer and tick the card together. Fix anything on the spot before leaving.

Step 6 · Within 1 hour

Photo report

Before and after photos plus the checklist, texted and emailed from the CRM with the tech's name.

Step 7 · Next day

Review ask + Club offer

A thank-you, a one-tap review link, and a Shark Club invite with the first-visit price locked in.

Crew culture

Ranks people want to earn

The rank bar on the chest shows experience. Customers notice it, and crew members have something to work toward. Ranks carry across every Shark company.

Pup

In training. Always works with a Hammerhead. Learns the Standard.

Fin

Certified tech. Runs jobs solo. Has passed the Fin Check audit.

Hammerhead

Crew lead. Trains Pups, closes upsells on site, owns a van.

Great White

Runs a company or a territory. This is who takes over when you open the next one.

The glue between companies

The Shark Club

Your recurring plans become a membership. A member of one Shark company is a member of all of them, so every new company opens with customers already waiting.

Shark Exterior rollout

How we switch the flagship over

Now · first 30 days

Look the part

  • Finalize the fin and wave lockup
  • Order 3 to 5 crew shirts with orange yoke, name patches and navy caps
  • Van door magnets plus a back-door fin decal
  • Restyle the CRM and quote tool to the house tokens
  • Print Fin Check cards and shoe covers
Next · days 30 to 90

Run the ritual

  • CRM sends the Fin's up text, photo report and review ask
  • Rename recurring plans to Shark Club tiers
  • Yard sign left for 48 hours after every job
  • Website restyled to match
  • Pup and Fin training checklist
Later · 6 months and on

Open the next company

  • CRM supports more than one company with a switcher
  • Promote a Great White to run Exterior
  • Launch Shark Electric or Shark Detail with the same kit
  • Club members get first access

Decisions only you can make

  1. Keep "Shark Exterior", or switch to "Shark Exterior Cleaning" on vans and signs? This plan keeps the short name.
  2. Third company: Detail, or Landscape? Both are designed to fit. Detail has the lagoon teal, Landscape has the kelp green.
  3. Uniform: is the colored-yoke crew shirt bold enough, or do you want an even louder signature item, like an orange bucket hat?
  4. Electrical needs a licensed master electrician on the license. Partner, hire, or get licensed yourself?
